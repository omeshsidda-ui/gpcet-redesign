## **Indoor Stadium**

[![](https://www.gpcet.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/indoor-stadium.jpg)](https://www.gpcet.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/indoor-stadium.jpg)The College has facilities of **Indoor Stadium** with

-   Ball Batminton Court
-   Batminton Court
-   Basket Ball Court
-   Table-tennis, Carrom and Chess
-   The Indoor Stadium is normally kept open in morning from **6 a.m to 8 a.m** and evening **4 p.m to 7 p.m** on all **working days** and from **6 a.m to 6 p.m on holidays**.
