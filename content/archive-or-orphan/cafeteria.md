## **Cafeteria**

In order to satisfy the appetites of the students and to give them a break after a day hard work, the institute campus is provided with a relaxing and comfortable canteen. The canteen not only provides the students of the campus with mouth watering dishes but is also one of the most happening places in the campus with all the students gathering there to discuss their new experiences in the campus. Healthy and hygienic meals are made in the college canteen by a professionally managed catering company.

This is place to rejuvenate the tired spirits through lip smacking delicacies. The cafeteria provides much needed appetizer breaks in a cozy atmosphere. The cafeteria services are available round the clock athighly reasonable prices and smile.
