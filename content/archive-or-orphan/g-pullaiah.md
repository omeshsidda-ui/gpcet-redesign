### Sri G. Pullaiah

SECRETARY

Starting his career as a Teacher in Zilla Parishad, he envisioned in establishing an Institution with Traditional values, he founded Ravindra Vidya Niketan, his cherished dream of incorporating Viswakavi Gurudev’s Shantiniketan.

Ever since it’s inception, that Institution has been expanding and today it has become a huge organization. He put his heart and soul into this organization. In 1996 he started Ravindra Junior College and produced many state Rankers.

Recognizing the importance of women education he started Ravindra Degree College for Women in 2001. As he envisioned Qualitative education, he started Ravindra Residential School and Junior College away from hectic hubbub and humdrum of city in a hamlet ‘Venkayapalli’.

He envisioned the technical changes which revolutionized the field of education and started G.Pullaiah College of Engineering & Technology. Realizing the importance of empowerment of Women he started Ravindra College of Engineering for Women.

The Govt. of Andhra Pradesh conferred on him Best Teacher Award for his accomplishments. He is a strict disciplinarian who has imbibed the traits of dignity, decency, decorum and punctuality. He is simple and modest. He is available to the student community round the clock. The motto of Institution ‘Join to Learn and Leave to Serve’ is true as he shed his luster on the student community. His philanthrophic attitude lighted many poor lives.
