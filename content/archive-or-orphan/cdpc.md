## Career Development and Professional Competencies

**Choosing GPCET will provide you with the opportunity to maximize your career prospects.**

The Career Development and Professional Competencies (CSDP) is an exclusive and dedicated program offered by the “Careers Development Center” at GPCET. The rise of globalization has opened up the Indian economy to the global arena, intensifying competition among industries. Thus, it is crucial to equip engineering students with the necessary skills in newer technologies and engineering methodologies to prepare them for jobs in multinational companies. CSDPaims to foster a conducive environment by engaging the combined resources of industry and academia through skill-building programs such as SkillUP and SkillBridge. These programs are designed in collaboration with industries to bridge the gap between training and placements commonly observed in academic settings. This approach facilitates improved interaction between industry and academia, enhancing relevant skills and fostering innovative thinking among students.

CSDPactively promotes Industry-Institute Interaction, striving to develop a world-class workforce in the field of science and technology by cultivating various skills demanded by the industry. While India has an abundance of manpower, there is a pressing need for well-equipped individuals with employability skills. It is imperative to establish avenues for close interaction between academia and industry throughout all stages of technology development, ranging from conceptualization to commercialization.

**Vision:**  
To establish an exceptional Employability Skills facility that serves as a connecting link between students and the industry.

**Mission:**

-   Generate skilled and employable workforce in alignment with industry requirements.
-   Provide a platform for experiential learning through projects and training.
-   Facilitate two-way communication between resources and the industry, fostering numerous opportunities.
-   Offer exposure to the latest technologies and create avenues for mutual growth.
-   Enhance the confidence of resources in terms of professional and personal skills, while providing abundant placement opportunities.
-   Support the placement cell by equipping students with industry-ready technical proficiency.
