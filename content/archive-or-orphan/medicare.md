## **Medicare**

A Health Centre is housed in the campus. Doctors visits the college for regular health checkup of students. Consultancy service is provided for students and staff with medical specialists. The students have to undergo compulsory medical check up before appearing for their examinations. The faculty and students are subject to a medical examination during the annual blood donation Camps arranged by NSS.  
To releave the students from strees and strain the college invites renowned psychiatrists, who interacts with the student community, counsels them and passes on the valuable tips. The team of Doctors specialized in orthopedics and general medicine treats the students to be fit for both academics and extra curricular activities. The continues vigilance of physiotherapists maintains the health standards of student communities.  
The Yoga Guru Sri. Venkata Rangam trains a students with yoga practices to make the body and soul fit for academic track.
