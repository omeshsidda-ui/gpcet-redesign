## **Vision & Mission**

**Vision:**

“To be the source of imparting quality education to civil engineers along with necessary skill, knowledge and personality in order face the challenges in the society.”

**Mission:**

\* To provide a platform for gaining knowledge regarding emerging technologies in the area of civil engineering.  
\* To inculcate critical and innovative thinking in the minds of young engineers in order to face the challenges of the society.  
\* To provide good ethical and moral values to the young engineers.
