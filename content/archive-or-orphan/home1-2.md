To prepare professionally superior and ethically strong global manpower in technology and management to serve the nation and the world in the 21st Century.

To strive hard in training the students with the current technology and motivate them to take up research problems and innovations associated with personality development programs to meet the challenges in this competitive world in an efficient manner.
