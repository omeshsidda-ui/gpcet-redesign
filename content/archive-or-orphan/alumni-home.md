## **Alumni**

GPCET is proud of its Alumni, who are well placed in hundreds of reputed National / Multi-national Organizations across the globe. Many of our alumni have successfully pursued their higher studies in reputed institution in India and Abroad.

Alumni Association has been a platform for the GPCETians to bring friends of same batch / seniors / juniors to meet and interact. By joining as a Member, you can access the database of all the alumni. You are welcome to participate in any value addition Programs for the benefit of present students. Please visit us whenever you have some leisure.

The activities of Alumni Association are planned and executed by a working committee. Alumni meets / reunions are being organized regularly at different places – College premises, Bangalore.Many of the alumni are visiting the college to meet their teachers, interact with students, deliver guest lectures, take training classes, and recruit students for their companies.
