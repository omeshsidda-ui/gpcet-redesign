## Self Learning

![](https://www.gpcet.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Learning-is-not-attained-by-chance-it-must-be-sought-for-with-ardor-and-attended-to-with-diligence.-Abigail-Adams.png "Learning is not attained by chance, it must be sought for with ardor and attended to with diligence. – Abigail Adams")

## **Curriculum for B.Tech Students at GPCET:**

-   -   Compulsory institute core courses for overall development
    -   Freedom for students to pursue topics of interest beyond the prescribed syllabus
    -   Value-added courses to expose students to software/hardware trends
    -   Work-experience courses to provide practical industry knowledge
    -   Emphasis on cultural education, career and employment courses, rural women empowerment, open elective courses, and social service

## **Curricular Components that Require Self-Learning and Boundary Expansion:**

-   -   Project work and FSITech talk and concept videos
    -   Internship programs for practical experience
    -   Buid-IT for competitive coding
    -   Innovation and product development activities
    -   Seminars and group discussions
    -   Lab courses utilizing software tools for simulation

## **Encouragement for Participation:**

-   -   Student competitions, hackathons, and technical paper presentations
    -   Scope for learning beyond the prescribed syllabus

## **Facilities Provided by GPCET for Supporting Self-Learning:**

-   -   YouTube channel with various videos
    -   Directory of 2000+ video lectures in 45+ subject areas
    -   Each course includes e-Learning/Early Learning Readiness Videos (ELRV) with accompanying instructional materials
    -   Resources like course descriptions, syllabi, lecture notes, concept video topics, tech talk topics, open-ended experiments, problems, and project ideas
    -   Tutorial question banks, definitions, terminology, model question papers, and previous semester examination papers
