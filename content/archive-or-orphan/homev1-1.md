## **Why G. Pullaiah College of Engineering and Technology?**

---

Established in 2007, G Pullaiah College of Engineering and Technology (GPCET) has spent almost two decades shaping future engineers through a steadfast commitment to academic excellence, innovation, and impactful research. Renowned for its high standards in teaching and learning, GPCET  has consistently delivered quality education and fostering industry-ready professionals. By combining a strong academic foundation with experiential learning and a culture of continuous innovation, the institute empowers students to excel in their careers and contribute meaningfully to society. Recognized for its outstanding academic outcomes and student success, GPCET has earned the trust of aspiring engineers and proudly lives up to its reputation.

## THE GPCET ADVANTAGE

****G Pullaiah College of Engineering and Technology,**** Kurnool was established in the year 2007 by a devoted group of eminent Academicians having a long and outstanding experience in educational system with a mission ‘Join to Learn Leave to Serve’. It was first founded in the year 2007, with a modest intake of 240 and has gradually transformed itself into an integrated multi-disciplinary technological institute offering UG and PG Courses with a total intake of 1110.

****Career-focused education**** – Connect with leading companies and prepare for real-world success.

****Ranked**** constantly for the past eight years in all premier ranking agencies nationwide. This illustrates positive performance and quality standards we maintain in teaching, research, employability and innovation.

****Dedicated**** faculty offer students an innovative and stimulating learning experience both inside the classroom and laboratory environment and within their community.

****Professional and support staff**** offers students every opportunity to succeed in their studies and extracurricular activities with specialized services tailored to their individual needs.

****Superior facilities**** are available to students, faculty and staff, from air-conditioned smart classrooms and labs to business innovation to sports installations and to good food service in cafeteria.

****Big placements**** greet our students with ample opportunities and software companies visiting the institute every year and successful in placing 90% of eligible students. 20% of the students are going for higher studies.

****Research initiatives**** among students and faculty, and Entrepreneurship drive among students, makes GPCET as one of the best institutions to enhance the performance of the students.
