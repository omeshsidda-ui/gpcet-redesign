## Autonomus Exam Section

The office of the Controller of Examinations was established in the year 2018 after the conferment of Autonomous status to the institute.

The responsibilities of the office are:

-   Preparation of academic calendar which includes all the academic related activities.
-   Conduction of  Internal Examinations  and Semester End Examinations .
-   Processing and announcement of results.
-   Issuing certificates such as Grade Sheet, Consolidated Statements of Grade, Provisional Certificate and Transcripts.
