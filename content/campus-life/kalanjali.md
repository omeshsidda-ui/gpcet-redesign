## **Robotics Club**

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns%3D%27http%3A%2F%2Fwww.w3.org%2F2000%2Fsvg%27%20width%3D%27168%27%20height%3D%27176%27%20viewBox%3D%270%200%20168%20176%27%3E%3Crect%20width%3D%27168%27%20height%3D%27176%27%20fill-opacity%3D%220%22%2F%3E%3C%2Fsvg%3E)

Robotics is the field that is intersection of varied engineering disciplines Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics & Communications and Computer science. Through this club, one will learn skills ranging from electronics, mechanics, controls, programming. This club aims at applying the knowledge we gain in the classroom through various lectures and other sources, in real-world conditions. It helps the students with the habit of Thinking of Technology practically.
