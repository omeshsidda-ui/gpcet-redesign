## Emergency Medical Care and First-Aid Services

Emergency medical care and first-aid services are available within the campus, with one Medical Practitioner and one Nursing Assistant. The medical center is equipped with general medicines, and first-aid boxes are strategically placed in laboratories with moving machinery and other key areas.

## Ambulance Services

The institute provides an Ambulance with comprehensive facilities for emergency services.

## Medical Emergency Procedures

In the event of a medical emergency, the medical center offers ambulance services and immediate medical attention. The institute has established a partnership with Omega Hospital in Kurnool, which is affiliated with the KMR Education Society, to ensure access to medical facilities. Omega Hospital is located approximately 2 kilometers away from the campus and can be reached within 10 minutes. All emergency cases are promptly referred to this hospital.

## Responsibility and Communication

The Principal, Doctor, Dean of Student Services, and staff members are responsible for addressing students’ medical concerns and promptly informing parents or guardians.
