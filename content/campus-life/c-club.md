## **C Club**

![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns%3D%27http%3A%2F%2Fwww.w3.org%2F2000%2Fsvg%27%20width%3D%27160%27%20height%3D%27170%27%20viewBox%3D%270%200%20160%20170%27%3E%3Crect%20width%3D%27160%27%20height%3D%27170%27%20fill-opacity%3D%220%22%2F%3E%3C%2Fsvg%3E)

The **motto** of the Club is to conduct **“C” programming** activities under the Club name and to imbibe the students with the Cream of logical abilities and bring them close to the programming technology. This aside in gaining edge on IT apart from their academics. The student dynamicity, inner personality, ideas and other skills can be excavated and to promote them to future technical world.
