## **Principal**

Much importance is given to the role of engineers in various applications involving Electrical, Electronics, Construction, Mechanical and Software. Globalization indeed offers great opportunities for the engineers to perform well on a larger platform and thus have a great scope of exhibiting their technical skills. The main factor that decides the production of such quality engineers remains in providing good engineering education In order to face these challenges, GPCET offers best engineering programmes in both Undergraduate and Postgraduate across the State. Ever since its inception, GPCET has concentrated on providing industry ready engineers to the society. Today the alumni of GPCET are well placed in reputed organizations all over India and abroad. The faculty and staff of GPCET are well qualified and eager to give quality education to the students. A well maintained campus also adds to the good academic ambience. Apart from the academics, many opportunities in the form of extra-curricular activities are provided for the students to develop further.
