## **Department of Mechanical Engineering**

## Overview

Mechanical Engineering Department, which started in 2013, is now offering B.Tech degree in Mechanical Engineering. The department has well-qualified, committed and motivated faculty. It has well-equipped laboratories, workshop, and other infrastructure. The department is also engaged in Industry- Institute interaction programme. From time to time, the department holds events such as symposia, short term courses etc.
