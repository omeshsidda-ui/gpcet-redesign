## ABOUT DEPARTMENT

The Department of Artificial Intelligence (AI) at GPCET symbolizes the GPCET tradition of excellence as a leader in Artificial Intelligence education and research.  CAI is in a period of exciting growth and opportunity. The department is dedicated to education, research and overall excellence. AI Graduates capture leading appointments in IT service industries, as well as fuel the Internet, wireless communications, and cross-disciplinary IT Industries. The AI program at GPCET was started in 2020. Recognized by AICTE and affiliated to JNTUA, Anantapur the current intake is 180 students.

## VISION

To deliver the qualitative, innovative and ethical computer science technocrats who strive for the benefit of society

## MISSION

**Nurturing the future leaders in academia, information technology, industry and entrepreneurial pursuit, through a contemporary curriculum of theory and application that develops the ability to solve problems individually and in teams.**
