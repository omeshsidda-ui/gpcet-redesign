Last year A part from their regular academics, the students inaugurated an R&D cell, where innovative research activities are carried out. They got trained under the  “DeskStream”. where DeskStream offers a unique and innovative desktop virtualization platform to deliver desktops swiftly and efficiently over the network where needed when needed. The user-centric platform combines all facets of ubiquitous computing from desktop to mobile on a single architectural platform to deliver a truly flexible computing experience to users. The product offers an impressive array of features and employs template technology to bring the web paradigm to desktop applications where a single copy of application is delivered to tens of thousands of users instantaneously.

By virtue of centralizing the entire lifecycle of desktop management, the platform keeps the personal part of users’ desktop on the network for on-demand native execution on any client device — the physical device along with its operating system is treated just as a utility. The platform is easy-to-deploy as integration is quick and seamless. . The department extended its support in the testing activities of the product.As the part of research activity, the students got trained in the latest Testing tools like Sikuli,Jython and Python and Testing automation Script languages etc.
